Frequently Asked Questions

How do I submit feedback?

To submit feedback, go to the Dashboard tab and click on "Submit Feedback". Select the meal type, rate the food quality and menu variety, add any comments, and click submit.

Can I edit my feedback after submitting?

Currently, you cannot edit feedback after submission. However, you can delete your feedback and submit a new one if needed.

How are the average ratings calculated?

The average ratings are calculated based on all feedback submitted by students. The system takes the mean of all ratings for food quality and menu variety separately.

Who can see my feedback?

Your feedback is anonymous to other students. Only the mess management team can see which feedback belongs to which student, and they may respond to your feedback.

How do I change my profile picture?

Go to the Settings page, and in the Profile Settings section, you can upload a new profile picture using the "Choose File" button and then click "Upload Picture".
